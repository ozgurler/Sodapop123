Privacy Policy

Soda Pop 1, 2, 3 — Thumb War Version · Last updated 1 September 2026

Soda Pop 1, 2, 3 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game works entirely offline and makes no network connections of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that gather data. We cannot identify you, and we receive nothing about you or your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings (chant speed, sound, haptics, left-handed layout, colourblind-safe mode) and your progress (bottle caps earned, thumb designs unlocked, stages cleared, win/loss counts) locally on your device only. This never leaves your phone and is not readable by us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. All fonts, artwork, and sounds are bundled inside it, so it works with no internet connection at all.

Children's privacy

This game is suitable for children. Because it collects no data whatsoever, there is nothing to disclose under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or similar laws. There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no external links, and no way for players to communicate with anyone through the app.

Purchases

There are none. "Bottle caps" are an in-game score earned only by playing; they cannot be bought, cost nothing, and have no real-world value.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
